Frequently Asked Questions

  • help How do I register for modules?

    First, create an account and verify your email. Then browse the module catalog, select your desired modules, and click the "Register" button. Make sure to check prerequisites and availability.

  • schedule When is the registration deadline?

    Registration deadlines vary by module and semester. Check the individual module details for specific deadlines. Early registration is recommended to secure your spot.

  • credit_card How much do modules cost?

    Module fees depend on the number of credits and category. Detailed fee information is available on each module's detail page. Contact the admissions office for financial aid options.

  • swap_horiz Can I change my module selection?

    Yes, you can withdraw from modules before the deadline through your dashboard. Some modules may have withdrawal fees or academic penalties, so check the module policies carefully.
